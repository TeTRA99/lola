Design System · v1.0

Warmth and clarity, turned all the way up for the people who need it.

Lola has two faces. Dad's surface is a giant, high-contrast button board in Spanish — voice is the real interface, the screen just needs to be unmissable under his thumb. The caregiver's surface is a calm, normal-density tool in English for teaching Lola the objects and rooms in the house. One system, two contrast budgets.
