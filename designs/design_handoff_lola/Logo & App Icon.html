Brand · Logo exploration

A friendly eye that looks with you, not at you.

Lola sees the world for Dad, so the mark is a lens — but a soft, rounded, approachable one, never a clinical eyeball. Three directions below; all read at 48 px on a busy home screen and double as the splash long-press target.

01

Three directions

Same rounded-square container, same blue. The difference is what lives inside the lens.
RECOMMENDED
Geometric lens
A clean iris with an off-centre catchlight — warm, alive, unmistakable as a tiny glyph. Simplest to render across every density.
Aperture
Six soft blades fan around the centre — a camera nod that says “I’m looking.” A touch more mechanical; great for motion (blades bloom open on launch).
Bloom
Concentric rings of light radiating from a core — the warmest, most “assistant” feel. Echoes the listening/speaking pulse in the app. Slightly softer at 24 px.
