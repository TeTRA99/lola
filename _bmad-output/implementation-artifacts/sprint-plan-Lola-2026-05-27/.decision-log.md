### Decisions made

- **Sprint length: 1 week** (Mon–Sun). Calibrated to a solo dev with no stand-ups — the natural review/replan cadence. 2-week sprints would lose granularity for a 6–8 week build.
- **Sprint count: 7 build sprints + 1 Pre-Sprint validation block.** The Pre-Sprint runs the 3 remaining days this week (Wed–Fri 2026-05-27 → 2026-05-29). Build sprints start Mon 2026-06-01 and run to Sun 2026-07-19. **Target sideload: Mon 2026-07-13** (Sprint 7 Day 1). Brief's hard ceiling is 8 weeks = Fri 2026-07-24.
- **Pre-Sprint contains V1–V4 validation + Apple Dev enrollment submission.** Enrollment has identity-verification wait time outside our control — kick off Day 1 so it clears by Sprint 6 (E6.2 finalize). Validation results lock NFR-9 (TTS fallback choice per platform) before E1 work starts.
- **Story sequencing follows the epics-and-stories §3 dependency graph strictly.** E1 ships before everything; E4 parallelizes with E2 in Sprint 3; E3 waits for E1.4 (STT, in Sprint 2) and E2.1 (prompt template, in Sprint 2); E5 sequences after E2/E3.
- **E1.6 Haptic adapter (L) deliberately held to Sprint 2** not Sprint 1, even though E1 dependency-wise allows Sprint 1. Reason: it's the biggest single story (1–2 days), it benefits from V3 results landing first, and bundling it with E2.1/E2.2 in Sprint 2 keeps Sprint 1's load conservative for the first week of real build.
- **Sprint 5 intentionally under-loaded** (~3 days of work vs. 5 available). First real buffer to absorb spillover from earlier sprints. Sprint 6 is the second buffer — sideload prep + manual E2E pass. Anything beyond 5 cumulative days of slip pushes sideload into Sprint 8 territory.
- **Sprint 7 is structurally different** — sideload Monday, then mostly observation + reactive bug fixes. Not story-counted because it's reactive.
- **Sideload target: Monday 2026-07-13.** That's the 6.5-week mark from today. Picked Monday so a full week is available for week-1 telemetry collection through Sunday 2026-07-19. Week-2 measurement window (per brief Success Criteria) is Sprint 8 territory.
- **No formal stand-ups.** Solo dev. The artifact's Plan/Actual checkboxes are the lightweight tracker; `bmad-sprint-status` updates them per sprint close. ClickUp Task statuses move as stories advance.

### Sprint composition rationale

For each sprint, picked stories such that:
1. All dependencies are met by the sprint's start (no story scheduled before its blockers).
2. T-shirt sum stays at or below ~5 day-equivalents for the 5 productive weekday slots.
3. Each sprint has a coherent **theme** that maps to a demoable user-visible state — even though demo is to Charly alone, having a "what does end-of-sprint look like?" answer keeps progress legible.
4. Heaviest single story (E1.6 Haptic, L) lands in the middle of its sprint so spillover has room.

### Sprint 1 task move — 2026-05-27 (later same day, after Sprint Folder fix)

- **Trigger:** Charly asked to *move* the 6 Sprint 1 tasks out of MVP into the Sprint 1 List (not add-to-multi-list, which the `SIML_001` cap blocks). The intent: Sprint 1 List shows the actual tasks; MVP no longer carries them.
- **API findings during probe:**
  - `PUT /task/{id}` with `list_id` returns 200 but silently ignores the field — task does not move.
  - No `/move` endpoint exists in v2 or v3 (probed 3 candidate paths, all 404).
  - `POST /list/{list_id}/task` with `parent` referencing a task in a different List returns `ITEM_137: "Parent not child of list"` — ClickUp requires parent and child to share a List.
- **Solution: recreate-and-delete pattern.** For each of the 6 tasks: GET → POST new in Sprint 1 (parent set to None) → DELETE original from MVP. The Epic parent relationship is *lost* in ClickUp's hierarchy (the 6 tasks are top-level in Sprint 1). Epic affiliation is now encoded in body text + `sprint:1` tag only.
- **Tag-on-create unreliability surfaced:** of 6 new tasks created with `tags: [...]` in the POST payload, only 2 actually carried tags after creation. Restored via `POST /task/{id}/tag/{name}` per missing tag — 100% success that way. **Lesson:** create task without tags, then apply via the dedicated tag endpoint per tag.
