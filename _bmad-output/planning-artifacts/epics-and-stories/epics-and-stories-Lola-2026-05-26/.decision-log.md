### Decisions made

- **Story count:** 30, expanded from the architecture's "~26" sketch. Two-thirds of the extra came from splitting "haptic adapter" (E1.6) and "Apple Dev enrollment" (E6.2) into their own stories rather than bundling — both have meaningful risk and timing externalities (CHHapticEngine debug; Apple identity verification) that deserve sprint visibility.
- **Estimation scale:** T-shirt (XS / S / M / L) calibrated to a solo dev's day. Avoided story points because the team is one person and points add false precision.
- **Aggregate estimate:** ~38 days, landing at the brief's 8-week upper bound with no slack. Flagged explicitly in §10 so sprint planning either budgets a buffer week or accepts the ceiling.
- **Dependency graph (§3):** Encoded the epic-level deps so sprint planning can run E4 in parallel with E2 and start E6's EAS/Apple-Dev scaffolding early. E3 sits after E2 (shared model plumbing). E5 sits after E2 + E3 (consumes their success paths).
- **Story format:** Each story has user-story line + PRD inheritance + Architecture inheritance + concrete acceptance criteria + estimate + dependencies + risk flag. This is the exact context shape `bmad-create-story` and `bmad-dev-story` consume per cycle, so no re-discovery during the build.
- **PRD AC traceability:** every PRD AC (AC1.1–6, AC2.1–5, AC3.1–5, AC4.1–5, AC5.1–3, AC6.1–4) appears in at least one story's acceptance list. Worked through this explicitly so the implementation contract from the PRD has no orphans.
- **Validation V1–V4 made a story (E6.5):** runs concurrent with E1 per architecture §10. Not parked, not informal — repo artifacts in `docs/validation/` are part of E6's exit criteria.
- **What I did NOT decide:**
  - Sprint count and per-sprint scope — `bmad-sprint-planning`'s job.
  - ClickUp task structure (Folder-of-Lists restructure mentioned in reference memory) — sprint planner's call.
  - Pin specific dates to stories — meaningless before sprint planning.
